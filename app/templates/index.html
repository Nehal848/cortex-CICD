How Cortex Heals Your Pipelines

1. Detect
Cortex hooks into Jenkins, GitHub Actions, and Kubernetes to instantly detect pipeline failures or pod crashes.
2. Diagnose
Our AI engine pulls the logs, parses stack traces, and predicts the exact root cause with high confidence.
3. Heal
The Autonomous Engine applies a healing strategy—restarting pods, rolling back deployments, or generating patch PRs.
4. Verify
Cortex monitors the health checks to ensure the fix was successful before resolving the incident silently.
